How it works

Laundry day, handled.

Three taps between you and a clean closet.

Link to your cleaner

Scan the QR code at your cleaner's counter, or enter their store code. You're set up in under a minute.

Pick a time

Choose a pickup window that suits you: morning, midday or afternoon. Make it weekly if you like.

Hand it off

Your driver collects it, your cleaner cleans it, and it comes back to your door. You get an update at every step.

Plans

Simple pricing for cleaners.

Every plan includes every feature and unlimited customers. They differ only in how many drivers you have and how many stops your drivers make each month.

Starter

$29.99 / month
  • 150 stops a month
  • 1 driver
  • Unlimited customers
  • Every feature included

Growth

$59.99 / month
  • 500 stops a month
  • Up to 3 drivers
  • Unlimited customers
  • Every feature included

Fleet

$129.99 / month
  • Unlimited stops
  • Up to 10 drivers
  • Unlimited customers
  • Every feature included

A stop is each time your driver stops the van to make a pickup or a doorstep delivery. Starter's 150 stops cover about 75 full pickup-and-delivery cycles. If you drop off several orders at the same house on one trip, that still counts as one stop. We'll let you know as you get close to your limit. A store that goes well past it has 15 days to move to a bigger plan, and we never stop a driver mid-route.

You apply in the CR Business app. We check your business license and call the number on your Google listing, then you subscribe through the App Store to open your store. Cancel any time in your App Store settings.
